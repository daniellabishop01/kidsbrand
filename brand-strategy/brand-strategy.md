> **We're the cool uncle, aunt or grandpa: the one who's seen a thing or two, always has a clever line, and makes you feel like you can do absolutely anything.**

## 5. Narrative for grown-ups: *the story behind the brand*

*For the website's "Our Story" and "The Science" pages, investor and retail decks, and pediatricians. Same heart, plain language.*

**The shift.** More kids than ever are struggling to focus and settle. About 7 million US children have a current ADHD diagnosis, and many more struggle without one. Half or more of these families have already tried natural options.

**What's at stake.** For families, this isn't a "focus problem". It's *every evening*: homework battles, big feelings, bedtime standoffs. And somewhere in the frustration, kids start to believe they *can't*. Parents want help that's gentle and natural, but the supplement aisle is full of hidden blends and big promises.

**The better future.** Kids who believe they *can*: calmer afternoons, homework done without a fight, room for imagination and creativity, and personalities free to shine. Every win celebrated. And parents who feel good about every ingredient they chose.

**How we get there.**
1. **Studied in kids:** made with **Bacozest**, a patented natural Bacopa extract (standardized to 20% bacosides), studied in a placebo-controlled trial with children ages 6–12.
2. **One ingredient, one clinical dose:** 160 mg in every stick, exactly as studied. Nothing hidden.
3. **Made for kids:** debittered, kid-chosen flavors, mixed into water once a day, the same way the study was done.
4. **Built to celebrate:** a reward chart, stickers and reminders that turn a daily routine into a daily win. Consistency is what makes it work; celebration is what makes it stick.

**Proof.** In the study, children taking Bacozest improved compared with placebo on focus, planning, memory and learning, hyperactivity and distraction, sleep quality, anxiety and aggression, and getting along with others, with no serious side effects reported. We hold exclusive US sourcing rights, so no other brand can claim this study.

### Short versions
- **For kids:** *"One stick, one sip, one fresh start. Ready? Let's do this. Together."*
- **For grown-ups (elevator):** *"Homework shouldn't be a battle. [Brand] Focus & Calm is one gentle, natural, non-stimulant ingredient, studied in kids ages 6–12 at the exact dose in every stick, to support focus, memory and calm. Kids love the taste, you'll love what's inside, and every win gets celebrated."*

**If [Brand] were a person:** the cool uncle, aunt or grandpa. The one with the best stories, the cleverest jokes and the twinkle in their eye. They show up, they sit right beside you, they remember every win you've ever had, and somehow they make you feel ten feet tall. Kids adore them; parents trust them completely.

| **Playful, with a twinkle** | Silly, goofy or babyish |
| **Confident and a little legendary** | Showing off, or making it about us |
| **Smart and honest** | Clinical, salesy or full of hype |

### How we tell stories *(our "cool uncle" playbook)*
1. **The kid is always the hero.** We're the wise one in their corner, never the star.
2. **No slang. Ever.** We win kids over with wit, not with trendy words. Clever lines last; slang goes stale by the next school year.
3. **Wordplay is our love language.** Twists on familiar phrases ("Small wins. Big deal."), surprising word choices, and lines that reward a second read.
4. **Speak up to kids, not down.** We assume they're clever, because they are.
5. **Tell it like a story.** Every message has a little beginning, middle and happy ending, the way a great grandparent tells it.
6. **Jokes on two levels.** Kids smile at one thing, parents smile at another.
7. **Heart under the wit.** Every line still says the same thing: *you can do anything, and you'll never have to do it alone.*
8. **Side by side, not from the sidelines.** We say "let's" and "together" more than "you should". Kids feel supported, not watched.

*Inspired by great storytellers (Pixar, Disney, Roald Dahl, Dr. Seuss), not by their characters or catchphrases. Avoid owned phrases and anything that looks like borrowed characters.*

### Speaking to kids, safely *(important)*
- **Talk to kids in the product experience** (box, sticks, stickers, chart, mascot, kids' corner). **Direct paid advertising and purchase messages to grown-ups.** Children's advertising is self-regulated by **CARU** (the Children's Advertising Review Unit), and collecting data from kids under 13 is governed by **COPPA**. Run kid-facing ads and the kids' corner past compliance review.
- **Always show a grown-up giving the product.** Never suggest kids take it on their own or "take more for more wins".
- **Never make it sound like candy.** Talk about great flavor, not treats, and keep packs child-resistant and stored out of reach.
- **Celebrate the habit, not the dose.** The Brag Board rewards the daily routine (one stick a day), never "more sticks, more stars".
