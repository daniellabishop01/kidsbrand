## 4. Values: *how we act*

| Value | What it means | What it doesn't mean |
|---|---|---|
| **Proof over promises** | Every claim traces back to a study, ideally in kids at the dose on our label. We show the research in plain language. | Overclaiming, "clinically proven" with the dose hidden, or borrowing science from studies in other age groups or other ingredients. |
| **Nothing hidden** | One clinical dose, no proprietary blends, a clear label. We tell parents what to expect, and how long it takes. | Fine print, vague "blends", or burying side effects or limits. |
| **Gentle by design** | Non-stimulant, made for growing bodies, tested by kids for taste. Safety comes first, every time. | Candy-like products, mega-doses, or anything that pressures a kid to take more. |
| **Celebrate small wins** | We cheer progress, not perfection: homework done, a calmer evening, a good night's sleep. Kudos is our language. | Labeling kids by struggles, or shaming parents for needing help. |
| **Better together** | Parents, kids and doctors on the same team. We encourage talking with your child's doctor and support the whole family's routine. | Positioning ourselves against doctors or medication, or making parents feel alone. |

## 5. Narrative: *our story*

*Structure: the shift in the world → what's at stake → the promised land → how we get there → proof.*

**The shift.** More kids than ever are struggling to focus and settle. About 7 million US children have a current ADHD diagnosis, and many more struggle without one. Half or more of these families have already tried natural options.

**What's at stake.** For families, this isn't a "focus problem". It's *every evening*: homework battles, big feelings, bedtime standoffs. Parents want help that's gentle, but the supplement aisle is full of blends built on borrowed science, hidden doses and big promises. Most parents end up guessing.

**The promised land.** Calmer afternoons. Homework that gets done without a fight. A kid who feels proud of their day, and a parent who feels good about what they chose.

**How we get there.**
1. **Studied in kids:** made with **Bacozest**, a patented Bacopa extract (standardized to 20% bacosides), studied in a placebo-controlled trial with children ages 6–12.
2. **One ingredient, one clinical dose:** 160 mg in every stick, exactly as studied. Nothing hidden.
3. **Easy for kids:** debittered, kid-chosen flavors, mixed into water once a day, the same way the study was done.
4. **Built for the routine:** a reward chart, stickers and reminders, because consistency is what makes it work.

**Proof.** In the study, children taking Bacozest improved compared with placebo on focus, planning, memory and learning, hyperactivity and distraction, sleep quality, anxiety and aggression, and getting along with others, with no serious side effects reported. And we hold exclusive US sourcing rights, so no other brand can claim this study.

**Short versions**
- **One-liner:** *[Brand] is the kids' focus and calm supplement proven in its own children's study.*
- **Elevator (for parents):** *Homework shouldn't be a battle. [Brand] Focus & Calm is one gentle, non-stimulant ingredient, studied in kids ages 6–12 at the exact dose in every stick, to support focus, memory and calm. Kids love the taste, and you'll love knowing what's in it.*
- **For kids:** *It's your daily boost for good days. Mix it, sip it, and earn your star!*

## 6. Positioning: *where we win*

**Positioning statement** (Arielle Jackson's format, which Kevan Lee recommends)

> **For** parents of school-age kids (6–12) **who** are struggling to focus and settle,
> **[Brand] Focus & Calm is** the daily kids' supplement
> **that** supports focus, memory and calm.
> **Unlike** blends built on borrowed science,
> **it's made with one ingredient, Bacozest, studied in children at the exact dose in every serving.**

**Brand-level positioning** (for when the range grows)
> **[Brand] is the proof-first daily wellness brand for growing kids:** every product kid-loved, honestly labeled, and backed by research in its age group.

**The building blocks**

| Element | Our answer |
|---|---|
| **Target customer** | Millennial parents (mostly moms) of 6–12-year-olds who research online and trust other parents. Three profiles: **the worried researcher** (child diagnosed or being evaluated, unsure about medication), **the frustrated parent** (undiagnosed child struggling with homework and big feelings), and **the achievement-minded parent** (healthy child under test or sports pressure). |
| **Need** | Relief for the whole family: something gentle, safe, proven and easy to stick with. |
| **Category** | Kids' focus and calm supplement (non-stimulant). |
| **Competitive alternatives** | Multi-ingredient focus blends (**FOCO Kids**, **OLLY Focus Buddies / Mellow Buddies**), calm products (**I'm Nutrients**, **Mighties**), all-in-one kids' powders (**Beam Kids**, **ZizZazz**), physician-formulated kids' lines (**Raise Them Well**), non-supplement aids (**NatPat** stickers), and doing nothing / waiting it out. |
| **Key differentiator** | The **only** product with its own placebo-controlled children's study, at the dose on the label, with an exclusive ingredient competitors can't claim. |
| **Key benefit** | Focus **and** calm in one (attention, behavior, mood and sleep were all measured in the same study), where competitors split them into separate products. |
| **Reasons to believe** | The children's study (n=40, ages 6–12, placebo-controlled) · Bacozest patent and standardization · exclusive US sourcing · no serious side effects reported · taste guarantee · pediatrician one-pager. |

**Messaging pillars**

| 🔬 Studied in kids | 🔍 Nothing hidden | 🌱 Gentle by design | 👨‍👩‍👧 Better days together |
|---|---|---|---|
| A real children's trial, not borrowed science. | One ingredient, one clinical dose. | Non-stimulant, made for growing kids. | Less frustration for kids and parents. |

**What we say and what we never say**

| ✅ Say | ❌ Never say |
|---|---|
| Supports focus, memory and calm | Treats or manages ADHD |
| Studied in children ages 6–12 | Alternative to Ritalin or stimulants |
| Talk with your child's doctor | Stop or reduce medication |
| Non-stimulant, gentle | "Clinically proven" (prefer "studied in kids"), or anything beyond what the study measured |

*Ads note: don't state or imply a child's condition ("Does your child have ADHD?"). Lead with situations ("Homework shouldn't be a battle").*

## 7. Personality: *who we are*

**If [Brand] were a person:** the favorite coach or cool aunt. Warm, funny, always in your corner, and they've done their homework. Kids want to hang out with them; parents trust them completely.

| We are… | …but not |
|---|---|
| **Playful** | Silly or babyish (we grow up with our kids) |
| **Smart** | Clinical or preachy |
| **Warm** | Sappy or sugary |
| **Honest** | Hype-y or salesy |
| **Encouraging** | Pushy or perfectionist |

**Voice by audience**

| Audience | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Parents** | Warm, clear, reassuring, a little witty. Plain language, short sentences. | *"Homework shouldn't be a battle. Here's what the research says, in plain English."* |
| **Kids** | Upbeat, celebratory, simple. Lots of cheering. | *"Homework done? Kudos! Add a star to your chart."* |
| **Pediatricians & practitioners** | Precise, evidence-first, no fluff. | *"160 mg standardized Bacopa extract (20% bacosides); placebo-controlled study in children 6–12; summary attached."* |

**Words we love:** good days, small wins, kudos, together, gentle, studied, simple, proud.
**Words we avoid:** cure, treat, fix, disorder (in marketing), miracle, "smart pill", hack, boost-your-IQ.
