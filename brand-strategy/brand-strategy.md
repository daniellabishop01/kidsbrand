## 5. Narrative: *our story*

*Structure: the shift in the world → what's at stake → the better future → how we get there → proof.*

**The shift.** More kids than ever are struggling to focus and settle. About 7 million US children have a current ADHD diagnosis, and many more struggle without one. Half or more of these families have already tried natural options.

**What's at stake.** For families, this isn't a "focus problem". It's *every evening*: homework battles, big feelings, bedtime standoffs. And somewhere in the frustration, kids start to believe they *can't*. Parents want help that's gentle and natural, but the supplement aisle is full of hidden blends and big promises. Most parents end up guessing.

**The better future.** Kids who believe they *can*: calmer afternoons, homework done without a fight, room for curiosity and creativity, and personalities free to shine. Every win, big or small, celebrated. And parents who feel good about every ingredient they chose.

**How we get there.**
1. **Studied in kids:** made with **Bacozest**, a patented natural Bacopa extract (standardized to 20% bacosides), studied in a placebo-controlled trial with children ages 6–12.
2. **One ingredient, one clinical dose:** 160 mg in every stick, exactly as studied. Nothing hidden.
3. **Made for kids:** debittered, kid-chosen flavors, mixed into water once a day, the same way the study was done.
4. **Built to celebrate:** a reward chart, stickers and reminders that turn a daily routine into a daily win. Consistency is what makes it work, and celebration is what makes it stick.

**Proof.** In the study, children taking Bacozest improved compared with placebo on focus, planning, memory and learning, hyperactivity and distraction, sleep quality, anxiety and aggression, and getting along with others, with no serious side effects reported. And we hold exclusive US sourcing rights, so no other brand can claim this study.

> **Kudos Focus & Calm is** a natural daily supplement

### Brand positioning *(as the range grows)*
> **Kudos makes natural, research-backed products that help children thrive:** kid-loved, honestly labeled, and made to help every kid's spark shine.

### Brand architecture
| Level | Example |
|---|---|
| **Master brand** | **Kudos** |
| **Hero product** | **Kudos Focus & Calm** (powder stick pack) |
| **Future range** | Kudos Sleep · Kudos Immune · Kudos Gut · Kudos Hydrate · Kudos Greens |
| **Teen line** | **Kudos Teen**: Study Days, Sleep, Sport |

### The building blocks

| Element | Our answer |
|---|---|
| **Target customer** | Millennial parents (mostly moms) of 6–12-year-olds who research online and trust other parents. Three profiles: **the worried researcher** (child diagnosed or being evaluated, unsure about medication), **the frustrated parent** (undiagnosed child struggling with homework and big feelings), and **the achievement-minded parent** (healthy child under test or sports pressure). |
| **Need** | Help for the whole family that's gentle, natural, proven, and easy to stick with. |
| **Category** | Natural kids' focus and calm supplement (non-stimulant). |
| **Competitive alternatives** | Multi-ingredient focus blends (**FOCO Kids**, **OLLY Focus Buddies / Mellow Buddies**), calm products (**I'm Nutrients**, **Mighties**), all-in-one kids' powders (**Beam Kids**, **ZizZazz**), physician-formulated kids' lines (**Raise Them Well**), non-supplement aids (**NatPat** stickers), and doing nothing / waiting it out. |
| **Key differentiator** | The **only** product with its own placebo-controlled children's study, at the dose on the label, with an exclusive ingredient competitors can't claim. |
| **Key benefit** | Focus **and** calm in one (attention, behavior, mood and sleep were all measured in the same study), where competitors split them into separate products. |
| **Reasons to believe** | The children's study (n=40, ages 6–12, placebo-controlled) · Bacozest patent and standardization · exclusive US sourcing · no serious side effects reported · taste guarantee · pediatrician one-pager. |

### Messaging pillars

| 🔬 Studied in kids | 🔍 Nothing hidden | 🌱 Gentle by design | 🎉 Every win counts |
|---|---|---|---|
| A real children's trial, not borrowed science. | One natural ingredient, one clinical dose. | Non-stimulant, made for growing kids. | Less frustration, more celebration, for kids and parents. |

### What we say and what we never say

| ✅ Say | ❌ Never say |
|---|---|
| Supports focus, memory and calm | Treats or manages ADHD |
| Studied in children ages 6–12 | Alternative to Ritalin or stimulants |
| Talk with your child's doctor | Stop or reduce medication |
| Natural, non-stimulant, gentle | "Clinically proven" (prefer "studied in kids"), or anything beyond what the study measured |

*Ads note: don't state or imply a child's condition ("Does your child have ADHD?"). Lead with situations and wins ("Homework shouldn't be a battle", "Homework done? Kudos!").*

## 7. Personality: *who we are*

**If Kudos were a person:** the favorite coach or cool aunt. Full of heart and zeal, the first to cheer every win, always in your corner, and they've done their homework. Kids feel like they *can* around them; parents trust them completely.

| We are… | …but not |
|---|---|
| **Fun and playful** | Silly or babyish (we grow up with our kids) |
| **Zealous and joyful** | Over-the-top or hyper |
| **Encouraging** | Pushy or perfectionist |
| **Warm, full of heart** | Sappy or sugary |
| **Smart** | Clinical or preachy |
| **Honest** | Hype-y or salesy |

### Voice by audience

| Audience | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Parents** | Warm, clear, reassuring, a little witty. Plain language, short sentences. | *"Homework shouldn't be a battle. Here's what the research says, in plain English."* |
| **Kids** | Upbeat, celebratory, simple. Lots of cheering. | *"Homework done? Kudos! Add a star to your chart."* |
| **Pediatricians & practitioners** | Precise, evidence-first, no fluff. | *"160 mg standardized Bacopa extract (20% bacosides); placebo-controlled study in children 6–12; summary attached."* |

**Words we love:** kudos, you can, spark, shine, celebrate, every win, zeal, thrive, together, natural, feel good, proud.
**Words we avoid:** cure, treat, fix, disorder (in marketing), miracle, "smart pill", hack, fuel, boost-your-IQ.

### The name in action
- **Reward chart:** *"Earn your kudos!"* (a star or sticker for every win)
- **Subscription:** *"Kudos Club"*
- **Reviews and community:** *"Give a kudos"*: parents share their kid's wins
- **Packaging:** *"Today's win: ______"* on the box flap for kids to fill in
